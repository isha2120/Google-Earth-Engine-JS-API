Hello! I am a third-year B.Tech student in Integrated Circuit Design and Technology at IIT Gandhinagar, focusing on analog, mixed-signal (AMS), and digital CMOS design.

My current design work in Cadence Virtuoso includes:

- 180 nm rail-to-rail input/output operational amplifier designed for a low-voltage audio driver.
- Replication and validation of a 28 nm floating-point SRAM compute-in-memory macro based on a recent IEEE paper\*.

Over the summer, I worked as a research intern at LP-CAS under Prof. Madhav Pathak. During the internship, I designed an on-chip resonant gate driver with charge recycling in 180 nm CMOS, which achieved 8× lower power dissipation and a 6.5× faster rise time than a standard totem-pole driver.

My earlier projects include a 48 V-to-12 V closed-loop synchronous buck converter with Type III compensation, and hands-on work with Verilog-based RTL design, FPGAs, and analog PCB layout. Beyond core coursework in semiconductor devices and computer architecture, my background includes practical training in IC fabrication processes and TCAD simulation using Synopsys Sentaurus.

I look forward to connecting with engineers, researchers, and students across IC design, AMS and VLSI, power electronics, and semiconductors. I am open to research or internship opportunities where I can apply my skills and continue learning.
